# Part A — Guide for visitors

## A1. What AskHub is and who it is for

AskHub is a free, public directory of opportunities for people building AI and data projects in Africa: cloud credits, courses, grants, accelerator programmes, open datasets, competitions and communities. It is run by the AI Hub for Sustainable Development, an initiative co-led by MIMIT and UNDP.

You do not need an account. Nothing is tracked about you personally. Every listing has been reviewed by the AI Hub team before it appears.

It is for you if you are a founder, a researcher, a student, a developer, or someone who supports any of those people — in any African country.

[Screenshot: the AskHub home page, showing the welcome band, the Browse menu on the left and the Recently added rail on the right]

## A2. The seven categories

Every resource belongs to one main category, and sometimes a second one.

| Category | What you will find |
|---|---|
| **Compute** | Processing power and cloud credits to train and run your models. |
| **Courses** | Courses and curricula to learn and build a skill. |
| **Funding** | Money to finance the work — grants, prizes and investment. |
| **Accelerators** | Structured, time-bound programmes that support a venture's growth. |
| **Data & Datasets** | Open datasets and benchmarks for building and evaluating AI. |
| **Challenges & Competitions** | Competitions and hackathons to test your skills on real problems. |
| **Community & Events** | Gatherings, chapters and events that connect the African AI community. |

## A3. Tags, and what "curated" means

**Tags** describe who a resource is for. They help you narrow the list; they never hide a resource from you unless you choose a filter.

- **Sectors** — Energy, Agriculture, Health, Water, Education & Training, Infrastructure. A resource with no sector tags is open to every sector.
- **Stages** — New to AI, Getting started, Building, Scaling.
- **Where it is open** — Global, All Africa, AI Hub programme countries, or a list of selected countries. The country filter offers the 18 AI Hub programme countries.

**Curated** means every resource was reviewed by the AI Hub team against two questions before it was published: *is this a genuine AI or data resource?* and *can an African innovator actually access it?* It does not mean the team has verified every detail with the provider, or that you are guaranteed to be eligible. Always read the provider's own page before applying.

## A4. Finding resources

### Browse by category

1. On the home page, look at the **Browse** menu on the left.
2. Click a category. The directory below filters to it, and the menu expands to show its sub-categories.
3. Click a sub-category to narrow further, or **More** to see everything in the category.

[Screenshot: the Browse menu with Funding expanded, showing sub-categories]

### Search in plain language

1. Click the search box in the header (or the one above the directory).
2. Type what you need in ordinary words, for example `funding for health AI in Kenya`.
3. Press Enter or click **Search**.
4. AskHub turns the words it recognises into filters — here, Funding, Health and Kenya — and searches the rest.

### Filter

1. Scroll to **All resources**.
2. Use the four dropdowns: **Categories**, **Sectors**, **Stages**, **Countries**.
3. Your choices appear as chips above the results. Click a chip's × to remove it.

[Screenshot: the directory with two filter chips applied]

### Sort

1. Use **Sort by** at the right of the results header.
2. Choose **Featured Resources** (the team's picks first) or **Recently added**.

### Page through and share

- Twelve resources show per page. Use the page controls under the grid.
- The address bar holds every filter, so you can bookmark a view or send the link to someone.

### Download the list

1. Apply any filters you want.
2. Click **Download CSV** or **Download Excel** at the top right of the directory.
3. The file contains the resources currently listed, with their names, providers, categories, description, link, tags and deadline.

## A5. Reading a resource listing

### On a card

[Screenshot: one resource card, annotated]

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Coloured banner with a name | The **provider** (the organisation offering it) and the resource's **title**. The colour is the category's. |
| Category pill | The main category; a second pill if there is a secondary one. |
| **Featured**, **Exclusive to the AI Hub**, **Early access** | Badges set by the team. |
| Grey tags | Sectors and where it is open. |
| Deadline text | See below. |
| **View details** | Opens the resource's page on AskHub. |
| **Apply** (or the provider's own wording) | Goes straight to the provider's site, in a new tab. Shown only while the resource is open. |

### On the resource page

- **Description** — what it offers and who it is for.
- **Who can apply** — stages, sectors, countries or scope, and the deadline.
- **Apply button** and **Share** (copy link, LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp).
- The line *Every resource is curated by the AI Hub team.*

### Deadline status

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Rolling** | No deadline; the opportunity is always open. |
| **N days left** / **Closes today** | The deadline is approaching. |
| **Closed** | The deadline has passed. Closed resources do not appear in the directory, but their page stays online so links keep working. The apply button is removed. |

## A6. Submitting a resource

### Before you start

Ask the two questions the team will ask:

1. Is it a genuine AI or data resource — compute, a course, funding, an accelerator, a dataset, a competition or a community?
2. Can an African innovator actually access it — is it open to applicants from at least one African country?

If both are yes, prepare: the resource's name, the organisation's name, a short description (what it offers, who it is for), the link where people apply, the deadline if there is one, and your own name and email.

### Steps

1. Open **Contact** from the header.
2. Click **Suggest a resource**.
3. Fill in **Resource name** and **Organization name**.
4. Choose the **Category / need**.
5. Optionally tick **Sector(s)** — leave them unticked if it applies to all sectors.
6. Tick the **countries** it is open to, or **Open to all**.
7. Write a **Short description** (up to 1,500 characters).
8. Paste the **Link to apply** — it must start with `https://`.
9. Enter a **Deadline**, or leave it blank for rolling.
10. Optionally add a **Contact email** at the organisation.
11. Enter **Your name** and **Your email**.
12. Click **Submit for review**.

[Screenshot: the Suggest a resource form, filled in]

### What happens next

- You see: *Submitted — the AI Hub team will review it before it goes live.*
- The team reviews suggestions on a fortnightly cycle, so expect a decision within about two weeks.
- If it is published, it appears in the directory. If the team needs more detail, they will write to the email you gave. You are not notified if it is not published.
- Your name and email are stored for that correspondence only and are never shown on the site.
- If you send several suggestions in a short time, you may be asked to try again in an hour.

## A7. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?** No. There is nothing to sign up for.

**Is AskHub free?** Yes. Some listed resources are free and some are paid; the description says which.

**Who chooses what is listed?** The AI Hub programme team reviews every entry against the two questions in A3 before it is published.

**Does "curated" mean the AI Hub has checked I am eligible?** No. It means the team judged the resource to be a real AI or data opportunity that African innovators can access. Eligibility for you personally is decided by the provider — read their page.

**I found a resource that is wrong or out of date. What do I do?** Use **Suggest a resource** on the Contact page and describe the correction in the description, naming the resource, or email the AI Hub mailbox.

**Why has a resource I bookmarked disappeared from the list?** Its deadline has passed. Its page still opens and shows Closed. If the provider reopens it, the team updates the deadline and it returns.

**Can I search in French, Portuguese or Arabic?** Not yet. The directory is in English at launch.

**What does the country filter do?** It shows resources open to that country. Resources open worldwide, across Africa, or across the AI Hub programme countries match every country you choose.

**My country is not in the list.** The filter offers the 18 AI Hub programme countries. Leave the country filter empty to see everything, including resources open worldwide and across Africa.

**Does AskHub track me?** No accounts, no profiles. If you suggest a resource, your name and email are kept for that review only.

**Can I download the whole directory?** Yes — clear the filters and click Download CSV or Download Excel.

**Can I get alerts when something new is added?** Not in this release. Bookmark the home page or sort by Recently added.

**Why is the apply button missing on some resources?** The resource is closed. The provider's page may still be reachable from the provider link.

**Is AskHub a partner of the organisations listed?** No. Providers are organisations whose opportunities are listed. AskHub itself is co-led by MIMIT and UNDP.

**Who do I contact about AskHub?** See A8.

## A8. Getting help

Write to the AI Hub mailbox shown on the **Contact** page and in the footer of every page. There is one address for everything: questions, corrections, and reports of problems with the site.

# Part B — Guide for administrators and curators

## B1. Roles and what each can do

| Role | Portal role | Can |
|---|---|---|
| **Site administrator** | admin | Everything below, plus: invite colleagues, change roles, deactivate accounts, set the analytics identifier. |
| **Content curator** | editor | Add, import, edit, publish, unpublish, close and delete resources; review and approve or reject suggestions; edit the home-page welcome copy. |
| **Reviewer/approver** | — | Not a separate role. Reviewing against the two gates and the quality standards is a duty of every curator and administrator before publishing. |
| **Viewer** | viewer | Read the Dashboard, Reach & Engagement, and Audit Log. Sees no editing controls anywhere. |

## B2. Signing in and account hygiene

### First sign-in (from an invitation)

1. Open the invitation email and click the link.
2. On **Set your password**, enter a password of at least 12 characters with a lower-case letter, an upper-case letter, a digit and a symbol. Enter it twice.
3. Click **Save password**, then **Go to the admin portal**.

Invitation links work once and expire. If yours says it is no longer valid, ask an administrator to send a new one.

### Signing in

1. Go to `/admin/login`.
2. Enter your email and password. Click **Sign in**.

[Screenshot: the sign-in screen]

### Forgotten password

1. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email, click the link, and set a new password as above. The link works once and expires after an hour.

You will see the same confirmation whatever address you enter; that is deliberate.

### Account hygiene

- Never share an account. Each curator has their own; the audit log records who did what.
- Sign out on shared devices (**Sign out** in the sidebar).
- An administrator deactivates an account the day a colleague leaves. Deactivated accounts cannot sign in and can be reactivated later.
- You cannot change your own role or deactivate yourself; ask another administrator.

## B3. Admin portal tour

[Screenshot: the Dashboard with the sidebar]

| Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Four counted tiles — Resources live (with pipeline count), Pending review, Expiring ≤ 14 days, Coverage (countries and sectors reached) — and Live resources by need. Every figure is counted from the database at that moment. |
| **Reach & Engagement** | A statement that reporting is not available yet. It will show visits and clicks once event capture is live. |
| **Resources** | The full table with tabs, search, filters, bulk publish, and the Add/Edit form. |
| **Review Queue** | Pending public suggestions, oldest first. |
| **Settings** | Analytics, Team access, Contact email, Welcome band. |
| **Audit Log** | Every change, who made it and when. |

On a phone the sidebar becomes a top bar with a **Menu** button; your account details and Sign out move to the foot of the page.

## B4. Reviewing submissions

### Open the queue

1. Click **Review Queue**. The count of pending suggestions is under the heading.
2. Read each card: badge (**New resource** or **Update**), title, description, organisation, category, link, who submitted it, and any programme contact.

[Screenshot: the Review Queue with one card]

### The eligibility checklist

Before approving, confirm:

- [ ] It is a genuine AI or data resource (fits one of the seven categories).
- [ ] An African innovator can access it (open to at least one African country, and the link works).
- [ ] The link starts with `https://` and goes to the provider's own page.
- [ ] It is not already in the directory (search the Resources table by name and provider).
- [ ] The description is factual and says who it is for.

### Decide

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Passes as written | **Approve & publish**. The resource is created live at once; if the organisation is new, it is added to the provider registry. |
| Passes but needs tidying (title, category, tags, description) | **Edit first**. The resource form opens pre-filled; correct it and click **Save resource**. Saving publishes the resource and approves the suggestion. |
| An **Update** suggestion about an existing resource | **Open resource to edit**. The existing resource opens with the suggestion beside it; make the change and save. |
| Fails either gate, or is a duplicate | **Reject**. |

The card leaves the queue as soon as its decision is made. The system records no reason for a rejection and does not notify the submitter.

### Standard reasons (for your own record)

Not an AI or data resource · Not accessible to African innovators · Duplicate of an existing listing · Link broken or not the provider's page · Insufficient detail to list · Provider requested removal.

## B5. Creating and editing a resource

1. On **Resources**, click **+ Add resource** (or **Edit** on a row).
2. Fill in the form. Required fields are marked with *.
3. Click **Save resource**. Click **Cancel** or × to discard.

[Screenshot: the Add resource form]

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Resource title** | The opportunity's own name as the provider writes it. No "!" and no year unless it is part of the name. |
| **Provider** | Start typing; pick from the suggestions if the organisation exists. If it does not, the note *New provider — added to the registry when you save* appears and the organisation is created on save. Check spelling: "Google" and "Google Inc" become two providers. |
| **Provider tier** | Strategic, Government, Development partner, Academic or Network. Internal; not shown to visitors. Use Network when unsure. |
| **Resource type** | Programme, Credits, Course, Community, Dataset, Competition, Fund or Report. |
| **Status** | Pipeline (default, not public), Live (public), Reference (kept, not public). |
| **Category** / **Secondary category** | The main category; optionally one more. |
| **Sub-category** | A short label such as "Cloud credits" or "HPC allocation". It appears under the category in the Browse menu. |
| **Stages eligible** | Tick every stage the provider accepts. At least one is required. |
| **Sectors eligible** | Tick the sectors the provider names; leave **All sectors** selected when it is open to any. |
| **Countries eligible** | Choose the scope. Only **Selected countries** shows the country chips; tick at least one. |
| **One-sentence summary** | One to three sentences: what it offers and who it is for. No superlatives, no claims the provider does not make. |
| **Action label** | The apply button's wording, for example "Apply now" or "Start the course". Default: Learn more. |
| **Deadline** | The provider's closing date, or blank for rolling. Never estimate. |
| **Banner image URL** | Optional `https://` image; leave blank for the category-coloured banner. No SVG. |
| **Application / registration link** | The provider's own page, `https://` only. |
| **Featured** | Pins the resource first in the directory and on the home page. Use sparingly. |
| **Exclusivity** | None, Exclusive to the AI Hub, or Early access. |
| **Sort order** | A number; lower sorts first among featured resources. Leave blank unless ordering matters. |

### Importing from the tracker sheet

1. Click **Import from tracker** on Resources.
2. Choose the CSV or Excel export of the tracking sheet (up to 512 KB, 500 rows).
3. Read the preview: each row shows Pass or Fail with a reason (missing title, provider or link; unknown category; insecure link; bad deadline; duplicate of an existing resource or of another row).
4. Note the count of new providers the file will create (at most 50).
5. Click **Import n resources** (n is the number of passing rows). Rows are imported one at a time, so one failure does not stop the rest. Imported rows arrive as Pipeline unless the sheet marks them pre-approved.

[Screenshot: the import preview]

## B6. Publishing, unpublishing, closing and reopening

| To… | Do |
|---|---|
| Publish one resource | Change its **Status** dropdown on the row to Live, or set Status in the form. |
| Publish several | Tick their checkboxes; click **Publish selected (n)** in the header. Already-live rows are skipped. |
| Unpublish | Set Status to Pipeline (not ready) or Reference (kept for the record). |
| Close | Nothing. When the deadline passes, the resource leaves the public listings automatically; its page shows Closed; the row moves to the **Closed** tab flagged *Auto-closed, past deadline*. |
| See what is about to close | Open the **Expiring soon** tab (deadline within 14 days). |
| Reopen | Edit the resource; set the new deadline, or clear it for rolling. It returns to the listings immediately. |
| Set an expected reopening date | Not available in this release. Put the expected date in the description if the provider has announced one. |
| Delete | Click **Delete** on the row and confirm. Refused while a public suggestion refers to the resource — decide that suggestion first. |

Every change appears on the public site at once; no rebuild is needed.

## B7. Managing categories and tags

- The seven categories, the six sectors, the four stages, the 18 programme countries and the four scopes are fixed lists. Changing them is a product decision implemented by engineering; it is not done in the portal.
- Sub-categories are free text on each resource. Keep them consistent: before typing a new one, look at what similar resources use in the table.
- Resource types are free text with eight suggested values; prefer the suggestions.
- Providers are created from the resource form (or import or approval). To rename a provider, ask engineering; the change follows through to every resource.

## B8. The fortnightly curation cycle

Target: 10–20 additions per cycle.

**1. Expiry sweep**
- [ ] Open **Expiring soon**. For each: confirm the date with the provider's page; extend or leave.
- [ ] Open **Closed**. For each: reopen with a new date if the provider has reopened; set Reference if it is over for good; leave if it may return.

**2. Queue**
- [ ] Clear the **Review Queue** using B4.

**3. Additions**
- [ ] Import the tracker sheet, or add resources by hand (B5, B9).
- [ ] Publish what passes review.

**4. Quality pass**
- [ ] Open the public site. Check each new entry's card and page against B10.
- [ ] Check the Dashboard's Live resources by need for any category falling behind.

**5. Sign-off**
- [ ] Record the cycle in the team's own log (date, additions, decisions). The **Audit Log** is the system's record.

## B9. Sourcing playbook

| Channel | How to find | How it enters the system |
|---|---|---|
| **Team-sourced logging** | Programmes, partners and contacts the team already works with | Log in the tracking sheet; import, or add by hand |
| **Active scouting** | Provider announcement pages, calls for applications, newsletters, community channels | Same |
| **Partner referral** | A provider or partner tells the team about an opportunity | Same; record who referred it in the team's sheet |
| **Public submission** | Anyone, via Suggest a resource | Review Queue |

The channel is not recorded on the resource in this release; keep it in the tracking sheet.

## B10. Quality standards and common mistakes

| Standard | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| Title is the provider's own name for it | Adding "2026", "Apply now!", or the provider's name to the title |
| Provider spelled as the registry has it | Creating a second provider by a typo or a variant ("AWS" and "Amazon Web Services") |
| Description: 1–3 factual sentences, who it is for | Marketing copy pasted from the provider; a description with no audience |
| Link is the provider's `https://` application page | A news article, a PDF, or an `http://` link (refused) |
| Deadline is the provider's date or blank | An estimated or "TBC" date |
| Tags only what the provider states | Ticking every sector "to be safe" — leave All sectors instead |
| "Democratic Republic of the Congo" in full | Abbreviating it |
| Nothing verified is claimed | Writing "verified" anywhere — the site's copy rules forbid it |

## B11. Troubleshooting and escalation

| Problem | Do this | If it persists, escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Invitation or reset link says it is no longer valid | Ask an administrator for a new invitation, or request a new reset link | Site administrator |
| Cannot sign in | Use Forgot your password; check with an administrator that the account is active | Site administrator |
| A control is missing (no Edit, no Add) | Your role is viewer | Site administrator, to change the role |
| "Something went wrong" on a screen | Reload once; sign out and back in | Engineering (a pending database change is the usual cause) |
| A resource will not delete | A suggestion refers to it — decide it in the Review Queue first | — |
| A suggestion will not approve: "missing an organisation, a category or a link" | Use **Edit first** and complete the fields | — |
| "A resource with this name already exists for that organisation" | Reject the suggestion, or edit the existing resource | — |
| Import rejects the file: missing columns | The sheet's header row must include at least the tracker's Resource Title, Provider and Proposed Category columns; rows without a summary or an application link are then rejected individually | Engineering, with the file |
| A public page shows old copy after an edit | Wait up to a minute and reload | Engineering |

Escalation order: content and process questions → the programme team; account and access → the site administrator; technical faults → the maintaining engineers, with the screen, the time and what you clicked.

## B12. Admin FAQ

**Who can publish?** Any curator or administrator. There is no separate approval step in the system; the review is the curator's own.

**Can I set a reopening date?** Not in this release. A closed resource returns to the listings when you set a new deadline.

**Why does the dashboard show no visits or clicks?** Engagement capture is not live yet. The screen says so rather than showing a number that is not real.

**Where is the reject reason recorded?** It is not. Keep it in the team's log if you need it.

**Is the submitter told the outcome?** No. If the team needs more detail, write to the email on the card.

**Can I rename a provider?** Not from the portal; ask engineering. Renames follow through to every resource automatically.

**What does the tracker import do to resources that already exist?** It skips them as duplicates and tells you. It never updates an existing resource.

**Why can I not change my own role?** So a single mistake cannot lock the team out. Ask another administrator.

**What happens when I change the welcome band?** The home page updates at once.

**Can a viewer see submitters' emails?** Viewers can open the Review Queue only if their role is raised; the audit log never shows a submitter's name or email.

**How do I know a change was made and by whom?** Open the Audit Log and filter by action, actor or date range.

**Is there a way to preview a resource before publishing?** Save it as Pipeline; only Live resources appear on the public site. The card and page look exactly as they will once Live.
