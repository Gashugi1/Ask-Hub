Privacy

AskHub is an open directory: you can browse and apply to every resource without creating an account, and we set no tracking cookies. If you contact us or suggest a resource, we use the details you provide only to respond and to review the submission. Questions: {{ contactEmailVal }}.

Terms of use

AskHub lists third-party opportunities in good faith. Eligibility, terms and awards are set by each provider — always confirm details on the provider's own site before applying. The AI Hub for Sustainable Development, co-led by MIMIT and UNDP, curates listings but is not a party to any agreement between you and a provider. Listings may change as programmes open and close.
